# Consumer Protection Act 2019

The Consumer Protection Act 2019 provides consumers with protections against unfair trade practices, defective goods, deficient services, misleading advertisements, and failure to provide promised remedies.

Consumers may seek redressal when goods are defective, services are deficient, or a seller or platform fails to honor reasonable obligations. Evidence such as invoices, order IDs, support communications, photographs, warranty documents, and delivery records can help establish the facts of the case.
