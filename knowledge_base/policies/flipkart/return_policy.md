# Flipkart Return Policy

Flipkart return and replacement options depend on product category, seller listing, return window, and product condition. Consumers should keep the invoice, unboxing photographs or videos, screenshots of the product page, support-ticket references, and pickup or delivery updates.

For wrong product, missing item, defective item, delayed delivery, or warranty-related disputes, the consumer should raise a support ticket quickly and document every response. If support closes the ticket without resolving the issue, the consumer can request escalation and a written explanation.
