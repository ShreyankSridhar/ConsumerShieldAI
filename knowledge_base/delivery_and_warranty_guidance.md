# Delivery and Warranty Guidance

Delivery disputes often involve non-delivery, delayed delivery, damaged delivery, missing package, or delivery marked complete without receipt. Useful evidence includes tracking history, delivery OTP records, address confirmation, support chats, and timestamps.

Warranty disputes often involve repair denial, replacement denial, service-center refusal, or disagreement about whether the defect is covered. Useful evidence includes invoice date, warranty card, diagnosis report, photographs, service-center job sheet, and support communications.

Consumers should first request a written reason for denial, then escalate with the marketplace, brand, courier, or consumer helpline depending on the disputed party.
